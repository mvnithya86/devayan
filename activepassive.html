50 Curated Questions from Foundational to Advanced ICSE/High School Standards

Level 1: Foundational Exercises Easy
Question 1Active to Passive
The cat chased the mouse.
Answer: The mouse was chased by the cat.
Question 2Active to Passive
She writes a letter every day.
Answer: A letter is written by her every day.
Question 3Active to Passive
The boy ate the delicious mangoes.
Answer: The delicious mangoes were eaten by the boy.
Question 4Active to Passive
My mother is cooking dinner.
Answer: Dinner is being cooked by my mother.
Question 5Active to Passive
They will complete the project tomorrow.
Answer: The project will be completed by them tomorrow.
Question 6Passive to Active
The cake was baked by Sarah.
Answer: Sarah baked the cake.
Question 7Passive to Active
The tree was struck by lightning.
Answer: Lightning struck the tree.
Question 8Active to Passive
The gardener waters the plants regularly.
Answer: The plants are watered regularly by the gardener.
Question 9Active to Passive
He has painted the front door.
Answer: The front door has been painted by him.
Question 10Passive to Active
The windows are cleaned by John every week.
Answer: John cleans the windows every week.
Question 11Active to Passive
The teacher praised the student.
Answer: The student was praised by the teacher.
Question 12Passive to Active
A song is being sung by the choir.
Answer: The choir is singing a song.
Question 13Active to Passive
We expect good news.
Answer: Good news is expected by us.

The thief was caught by the police.
Answer: The police caught the thief.
Question 15Active to Passive
The children broke the window pane.
Answer: The window pane was broken by the children.
Level 2: Intermediate Syntactic Shifts Medium
Question 16Active to Passive (Ditransitive)
The manager gave the employee a bonus.
Answer: The employee was given a bonus by the manager. / A bonus was given to the employee by the manager.
Question 17Active to Passive (Continuous)
They were repairing the bridge when I passed by.
Answer: The bridge was being repaired by them when I passed by.
Question 18Active to Passive (Interrogative)
Did you complete the assignment before the deadline?
Answer: Was the assignment completed by you before the deadline?
Question 19Active to Passive (Modal)
You must submit the application forms immediately.
Answer: The application forms must be submitted by you immediately.
Question 20Active to Passive (Imperative)
Switch off all the lights before leaving.
Answer: Let all the lights be switched off before leaving. / You are ordered/requested to switch off all the lights before leaving.
Question 21Active to Passive (Interrogative)
Who invited you to the party?
Answer: By whom were you invited to the party?
Question 22Active to Passive (Prepositional Verb)
The students laughed at the old man.
Answer: The old man was laughed at by the students.
Question 23Active to Passive (Perfect)
The scientists have discovered a new cure for the disease.
Answer: A new cure for the disease has been discovered by the scientists.
Question 24Passive to Active
A beautiful story was being narrated to us by our grandmother.
Answer: Our grandmother was narrating a beautiful story to us.
Question 25Active to Passive (Imperative)
Do not post the letters today.
Answer: Let the letters not be posted today.
Question 26Active to Passive (Modal Perfect)
They should have informed the police about the accident.
Answer: The police should have been informed about the accident by them.
Question 27Active to Passive (Interrogative)
Which book did the teacher select for the course?
Answer: Which book was selected by the teacher for the course?
Question 28Passive to Active
The matches were canceled due to inclement weather conditions.
Answer: The authorities canceled the matches due to inclement weather conditions.
Question 29Active to Passive
The sudden noise frightened the horse.
Answer: The horse was frightened by the sudden noise.
Question 30Passive to Active
Why was such a crude remark made by your friend?
Answer: Why did your friend make such a crude remark?
Level 3: Advanced Challenges (ICSE Pattern) Hard
Question 31Active to Passive (Complex Clause)
People believe that the ancient ruins are haunted.
Answer: It is believed that the ancient ruins are haunted. / The ancient ruins are believed to be haunted.
Question 32Active to Passive (Infinitive)
It is time to close the window counters.
Answer: It is time for the window counters to be closed.
Question 33Active to Passive (Imperative Negative)
Never insult the underprivileged.
Answer: Let the underprivileged never be insulted. / The underprivileged should never be insulted.
Question 34Active to Passive (Quasi-Passive)
This surface feels smooth.
Answer: This surface is smooth when it is felt.
Question 35Active to Passive (Factitive Object)
The people elected him President by a vast majority.
Answer: He was elected President by the people by a vast majority.
Question 36Active to Passive (Gerund Construction)
I remember my father taking me to the theater.
Answer: I remember being taken to the theater by my father.
Question 37Active to Passive (Intransitive Phrasal)
The car ran over the innocent pup.
Answer: The innocent pup was run over by the car.
Question 38Active to Passive (Implied Agent)
One should keep one's promises under all circumstances.
Answer: Promises should be kept under all circumstances.
Question 39Active to Passive (Bare Infinitive)
We saw the criminal escape through the back window.
Answer: The criminal was seen to escape through the back window.
Question 40Active to Passive (Complex Object)
They forced the captain to sign the resignation letter.
Answer: The captain was forced to sign the resignation letter.
Question 41Passive to Active (No Explicit Agent)
My pockets have been picked.
Answer: Someone has picked my pockets.
Question 42Active to Passive (Double Object Variation)
The headmaster taught us Latin.
Answer: We were taught Latin by the headmaster. / Latin was taught to us by the headmaster.
Question 43Active to Passive (Sensory Verb)
I heard her singing a classical melody.
Answer: She was heard singing a classical melody.
Question 44Active to Passive (Quasi-Passive)
Quinine tastes bitter.
Answer: Quinine is bitter when it is tasted.
Question 45Active to Passive (Noun Clause Object)
We expect that they will win the tournament.
Answer: It is expected that the tournament will be won by them.
Question 46Active to Passive (Imperative Advice)
Help the needy and the helpless.
Answer: The needy and the helpless should be helped.
Question 47Passive to Active (Implied Authority)
The lawbreakers will be severely dealt with.
Answer: The authorities will deal severely with the lawbreakers.
Question 48Active to Passive (Prepositional Nuance)
Smoke filled the room.
Answer: The room was filled with smoke. (Note: "with", not "by")
Question 49Active to Passive (Prepositional Nuance)
This news pleases me highly.
Answer: I am highly pleased with this news. (Note: "with", not "by")
Question 50Active to Passive (Complex Double-Clause)
They sent the letter and expected a quick reply.
Answer: The letter was sent and a quick reply was expected by them.
